# Pane privacy policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

Pane is a home screen (launcher) app for Android. This policy explains what Pane can see on your
phone and what it does with it.

## The short version

Pane doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share any of your data. Everything Pane reads
stays on your phone and is used only to show it on your home screen. Pane has no accounts, no
ads and no analytics.

## What Pane can read, and why

Each of these is optional. Pane asks for them during setup, and you can turn them off any time in
Android's settings or in Pane's Settings › Setup & permissions.

| Access | What Pane uses it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** (notification access) | Showing new messages, what's playing and unread counts on live tiles. Notification content is kept in memory only while it's shown. |
| **Calendar** | Showing your next events on the calendar tile. |
| **Approximate location** | Showing the weather for your area on the weather tile, only while Pane is open. |
| **Photos** | Showing a slideshow on the Photos tile. The Google Play version only sees photos you choose with Android's photo picker. Pane works out which photos look good, and where faces are, on the phone; nothing is uploaded. |
| **Usage access** | Arranging your Start screen around the apps you use most (auto layout). |
| **The list of installed apps** | Showing your apps in the app list and on tiles, as every launcher does. |

## Network use

Pane connects to the internet only to fetch the weather for the tile. That request contains your
approximate location (rounded coordinates) and goes to the free Open-Meteo service
(open-meteo.com), with no account or identifier attached.

## Crash reports

If Pane crashes, a report is saved on your phone. It never leaves the phone unless you choose to
share it yourself.

## Backups

Pane's settings and Start layout can be included in your phone's own backup (for example, Google
backup), as with any app. You can also make a backup file yourself in Settings › Backup & restore;
it's saved wherever you choose.

## Children

Pane isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone.
