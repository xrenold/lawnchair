# Pane: Play Store listing

Paste these into Play Console › Grow › Store presence › Main store listing.

**App name** (30 max)
Pane

**Short description** (80 max)
A live tile Start screen for Android. Quiet, fast, and everything stays on your phone.

**Full description** (4000 max)

Pane turns your home screen into a Start screen of live tiles.

Tiles come alive with what matters: new messages and who sent them, what's playing, your next event, the weather where you are, your next alarm and a slideshow of your photos. Everything else stays calm until you need it.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Small, medium, wide and large tiles. Hold one to resize, move or recolour it.
• Solid colour tiles, or see-through tiles that show your background as you scroll.
• Tile colours from your wallpaper, a mix of shades, or classic accent colours.
• Your own photo or a generated gradient as the background, with a gentle parallax.
• Auto layout: hold the arrow at the end of Start and Pane arranges your tiles around the apps you use most.
• Lock the layout when it's just right.

LIVE TILES
• Messages, calls and app notifications flip onto their tiles, with counts.
• Music tiles show the album art and what's playing.
• Calendar, weather, clock and photos tiles fill themselves in.
• Swipe a tile sideways to read its notifications and reply right there.

AN APP LIST THAT KEEPS UP
• All your apps, A to Z, with a letter grid to jump through them.
• Search as you type. Pin any app or shortcut to Start.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
• Everything Pane reads stays on your phone. The only thing it fetches is the weather for your approximate area.
• Every permission is optional and explained before Android asks.

Pane is open source (GPL-3.0) and built on the Lawnchair launcher and Android's own Launcher3.
